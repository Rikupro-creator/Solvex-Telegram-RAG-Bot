Users can create an account on Solvex by signing up with a username, email, and password.
All user data is stored securely in a database managed by the AI system.
Once logged in, users can access their dashboard, wallet, marketplace, and profile.

The Solvex wallet allows users to deposit and withdraw SLVX tokens.
To deposit SLVX, users copy the platform wallet address and send tokens from their personal wallet like Phantom or Solflare.
After sending tokens, users submit a deposit request with their sender wallet address, transaction ID, and the amount sent.
The AI system verifies every deposit by checking the transaction on the blockchain before crediting the balance.
Deposits are marked as pending until the AI confirms the transaction is valid and matches the submitted details.
Once verified by the AI, the SLVX balance is credited to the user's Solvex wallet.

For withdrawals, users enter their personal wallet address and the amount of SLVX they want to withdraw.
The AI processes all withdrawal requests automatically after verification.
For security, the AI will only send withdrawals to wallet addresses that have previously deposited to the platform.
This security measure prevents theft if someone's Solvex account is compromised.
If a hacker gains access to an account, they cannot withdraw to a new wallet that hasn't deposited before.
Withdrawal requests are processed automatically by the AI system once security checks pass.

The AI Marketplace is where users can discover and purchase AI-powered services.
Sellers can list their AI services with custom pricing set in SLVX tokens.
Buyers can browse services by category including Code Assistance, Text Generation, Data Analysis, and Creative Work.
Each service listing shows the AI model used, price in SLVX, description, and seller information.

To hire an AI service, buyers click on a listing and submit their request with details of what they need.
Buyers can attach files to their request if needed for the service.
After submitting, the order goes into Pending Review status while the AI reviews the request.
The AI reviews all orders and either Approves or Declines them based on the request validity.
If approved, the order status changes to Approved and the buyer can proceed to payment.
If declined, the buyer sees the reason for decline and can browse other services.
Once payment is confirmed, the order moves to In Progress status.
When the work is completed, the order is marked as Completed and the buyer receives their deliverable.

Sellers can create AI services by going to their Dashboard and clicking Create Service.
Sellers choose which AI model powers their service from options like Grok, Claude, GPT-4, Gemini, Llama 3, or Mistral.
Sellers set their own prices in SLVX tokens for their services.
Sellers write a description of what their AI service does and what buyers will receive.
Sellers select a category for their service to help buyers find it in the marketplace.
Once created, the service appears on the marketplace for buyers to discover and purchase.
